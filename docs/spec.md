# MVP spec

Status: draft v0.3 (2026-10-03). All open questions resolved; see [open-questions.md](archive/open-questions.md). MVP plan and calendar: section 18.

## 1. Goal
Pairs who play padel in a city (starting with Győr) get a fixed weekly match against opponents at their level, with fair rules on no-shows. The organizer has almost no admin work.

## 2. Roles
| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Visitor | See the landing page, the rules and the privacy page; send feedback (section 19). No standings, results or profiles (D134) |
| Player | Register, form a pair, join a league, propose and accept slots, record results |
| Organizer ("Szervező") | Per league: create and configure seasons, enter or take out a pair, seed groups, publish rounds, approve substitutes, settle disputes, override results, withdraw pairs. Edits the clubs (venues) of the league's city |
| Platform admin ("Admin") | Create cities/leagues, appoint organizers, send broadcasts, see traffic, usage and users, read feedback (Peter) |

Organizer and platform admin are roles on a normal player account, inside the app. The current `/admin` (interest list, broadcasts) moves into the app under the platform admin role, and Cloudflare Access is retired (2026-10-03).
- Code names: `player`, `league_admin`, `superadmin`; UI labels: Játékos, Szervező, Admin (2026-10-04).
- Organizer rights are granted per league (a row per league and account), so one account can organize one league and not another.
- Platform admin is a flag on the account, set only by a CLI command; no screen can grant or remove it. Peter's platform admin account is separate from the account he plays and organizes Győr with.
- Design and delivery: [archive/admin-plan.md](archive/admin-plan.md).
- Player status on the admin tables (decision log, 2026-10-31, D56): "Felhasználók", "Játékosok" and "Párok" show, per player, whether they have an account and finished the first-login page, a missing level or phone number, their active pair in the league and its entry in the league's current or next season (or "Nem nevezett" while there is one). The phone number itself, tap to call (D126), is on "Felhasználók" (the Admin's), "Párok" and "Játékosok", so the organizer can call players; never on a public page. Only the Admin sees each account's last login (the time of its newest session, recorded at every login). "Felhasználók" can be filtered (decision log, 2026-11-02, #151): by role, by registration ("Félbehagyta a regisztrációt" / "Regisztrált"), by missing data ("Nincs szintje" / "Nincs telefonszáma") and by pair and entry ("Nincs párja" / "Nem nevezett" / "Nevezett"), together with the search. One option per group; each option shows how many accounts it matches with the other filters and the search applied; the filters and the search are in the page address.
- **The people pages** (decision log, 2026-11-11, D155, D156, #301): one per-league page, **Játékosok** (`/admin/leagues/:league/players`, the league's Szervező and the Admin), lists every registered player of the league once (D128): name, level, phone (tap to call), "Párt keres", the pair's partner ("Nincs párja" without one), the entry in the current or next season, and what's missing. Filters, in the address with the name search (the player's or the partner's name): "Nincs párja", "Párt keres", "Van párja", "Nevezett", "Nem nevezett" (no approved entry, with or without a pair), "Hiányzó adat"; each shows how many players it lists. Under "Párt keres" each player shows since when they look, sorted by level and then the longest waiting first (as Párkeresők did). Two players without a pair can be ticked and put in a pair ("Párba teszem", section on "Párt keresek"). For the Admin, "Levél nekik" opens the broadcast page for the players shown. "Párok" lists only the pairs (the players without a pair moved to Játékosok; its old "Nincs párja" address redirects there), and "Párkeresők" is gone (its address opens Játékosok with "Párt keres"). The Admin also has "Felhasználók", every account. Each of these sidebar links has a short second line saying what the page lists ("Mindenki egyszer, párral", "A párok és nevezésük", "Minden fiók, adatlappal"). **The player's profile** (`/admin/users/:id`, Admin only; a Szervező gets 403 and sees names without a link): every player's name on the admin pages (Felhasználók, Játékosok, Párok and its strikes, Nevezések, Csoportok, Meccsek and a match's page with its log, Viták and Döntés, Helyettesek, the Szervezők on Ligák and Áttekintés, Napló's "Ki" and an account named as "Min") opens it for the Admin; an anonymized account's name stays plain text, as do names in a select option, a page or dialog title, a line that is itself a link, and the broadcast page's list of recipients (names with addresses, for checking the audience). It shows the name, email, phone, level (and whether it is locked), sex, role, login method, registration and last login, the status chips; per league the active pair with Párok's actions ("Benevezés", "Kivétel a szezonból", "Partner cseréje", "Felbontás", the same server functions as on Párok), or "Nincs párja" with the "Párt keresek" switch and "Párba teszem"; every pair of theirs with its dates and season entries; the email log ("Levelek", section 13, D157); and the Napló entries about them (written by them, naming their account, or about their pairs and entries; all of them on Napló with `?person=`). "Levél neki" opens the broadcast page for that player alone. The Admin can change the name, phone, level and sex ("Adatok módosítása"): the same checks as the player's own profile, but a field the player never filled in may stay empty, and one that has a value can't be emptied; a locked level (D79) stays locked for the Admin too. The email address can't be changed here. Each save that changes something is one Napló entry (`user.update`) naming the fields that changed, never their values; the player isn't notified. An anonymized or deleted account has no profile (404). **Flag for human review:** personal data on an admin page, editing someone else's account.
- "Jelentkezők" (the interest list, its "Hol tartanak" stages D57 and its CSV export D58), the poster page and the registration invites are gone with the interest list's flows (decision log, 2026-11-07, D129, #333): `/admin/signups`, `/admin/poster` and `/admin/export.csv` lead to the admin home (for the Admin `/admin/leagues`), and the menu has no Jelentkezők. The admin searches ignore case and accents.

## 3. Authentication
- **No passwords** (2026-10-04): log in with Google, or with a one-time 6-digit code sent by email (valid 10 minutes, at most 5 tries, single use). Nothing to hash, reset or leak. The code's record (the address, the hashed code, its expiry, the tries and the codes asked this hour) is deleted when the code is used, or by the cron once the code expired an hour ago and the hour-long code window ended (decision log, D99, #168).
- Sessions last 90 days and are extended while used; platform admin sessions 12 hours. Logout, and logout on every device.
- Anyone can create an account, invited or not (decision log, 2026-10-28, #113; replaces invite-only until 2 Nov): the first login of a new address, with Google or the emailed code, creates it. The bot check on sending a code and the code limits stay; a new address gets no code when it bounced or complained before, and codes to new, uninvited addresses may use at most a quarter of the daily email limit, 1,250 with the relay (decision log, D49, D51). The code step looks the same either way, also after a wrong code or a resend (an address that gets no email still gets a code nobody knows, under the same limits), and its "Nem jött meg?" line names the other ways in for everyone: the spam folder, another address, Google login (when set up) and the contact address as a `mailto:` link (decision log, D65, #123). A new player goes through the same first-login page. A new player starts without a pair; Kezdőlap and Profil's Párod section give them their own pair link in each of their leagues (section 4), and Párod says that the organizer finds a partner for those with nobody, with the contact address; a Szervező pairs them on the Párok page (section 4). Registration has its own page, `/register` ("Regisztráció"): the login flow with its own words (Google button "Regisztráció Google-lel", code step "Tovább"), linked from the landing page (each league's button with `?league=<slug>`, section 4) and the pair link; `/login` and `/register` link to each other (decision log, 2026-10-31).
- First login asks for a name and privacy consent.
- The first-login page prefills nothing from the interest list since #333 (D129; the prefill of D52 and the partner's address field of #96 went): a Google login brings its name, the rest is typed.
- Facebook login: after the MVP (Meta business verification and app review take weeks).
- No onboarding from the interest list (D129, #333): no registration invite goes out, and no pair forms from a signup. A player joins a league through its register button or chooses one (section 4, D128) and finds a partner with the pair link or "Párt keresek".
- Same email = same account, whichever way they log in. A Google login links to an existing account only if Google reports the email as verified.
- Profile:
  - Required: name, email.
  - Required: self-assessed level (kezdő / középhaladó / haladó / versenyző; asked at first login; accounts that predate it are prompted on the home page, and the level is required to apply for a season).
  - Required: phone number (Hungarian numbers only, stored as +36…, shown as `+36 30 123 4567`; visible only to opponents in the current round (with the email address, D135), to the player's active partner (D121) and to the organizers (D126: the league's Szervező on Párok and Játékosok, the Admin also on Felhasználók and the player's profile), never public; asked at first login, not on the interest form; it can be changed but not removed on the profile page; decision log, 2026-10-28). Name and phone are editable on the profile page; so is the level, until the player takes part in a season (decision log, 2026-11-02, #135): once one of their pairs was placed in a published group, the profile shows the level read-only with a short note, and any attempt to change it is refused (a hand-made form post gets a Hungarian error). No organizer or Admin override. The profile also shows the player's Élő-pont, with a one-tap explanation that links to the Élő section of the public rules (`/rules`, section 8); until the per-match update exists (#13) it is the starting value of the level.
  - Required: sex, "Férfi" or "Nő" only (#133; decision log, 2026-10-29, D54): what the player picks, never inferred from a name or prefilled; asked at first login next to the phone number and editable (not removable) on the profile page. An account from before it was asked is sent once, on its next app page, to a one-question page ("Még egy kérdés") and then on to where it was going. Never public and never shown to other players; it lets the organizer email a group of players (e.g. a women-only event; filtering broadcasts by it comes later).
  - The pair, "Párod" (decision log, 2026-11-08, D141, #346; until then a page and menu item of its own, "Párom"): a section at the top of Profil's default "Játékosprofil" tab, above the summary, with what Párom showed and under the same rules: the active partner's name, email and phone (tap to write or call, D121), "A párotok oldala" once the pair has a profile, the season entry; without a pair the pair link (D55) and "Párt keresek" (D59). One card per league of the player (D128), each naming its league; with one league one card without the league's name. The old address `/pair` leads to `/profile#parod` with its query kept (`/pair?joined=1` → `/profile?joined=1#parod`), so links in mail and notices sent before still work; every link the app makes points to `/profile#parod`. Neither the sidebar nor the phone's bottom bar has Párom any more; Profil stays.
  - Former pairs, "Korábbi párjaid" (decision log, 2026-11-08, D142, #347): under Párod, only when there is one, the player's dissolved pairs that played in a published group (the rule that gives a pair its profile, section 14), newest ended first. Each row: the former partner's name as a link to the pair's page ("Törölt játékos" without a link for a deleted or anonymized account), the league, the seasons and rounds it played ("2026/27 tél, 1–2. kör"), how it ended and the day (`2026. 12. 21.`, Budapest). "Partnercsere" when a Szervező's "Partner cseréje" ended it (its `pair.change` audit entry names the old pair), "Felbontva" otherwise (a dissolve, an account deletion). Never a former partner's email or phone.
- GDPR:
  - Privacy notice (section 17) and consent at registration.
  - Account deletion with anonymization: match history stays, the name becomes "Törölt játékos".
  - On request (an email to the contact address), the Admin deletes an account on "Felhasználók" with "Törlés" (#152; decision log, 2026-11-02, D60). The confirmation names the account and says what happens. Without history (no pair of theirs was ever placed in a published group, where every match comes from, and they were never an approved substitute, D109) the account is removed completely, with its pairs, entries, draft group places, partner search, pair links, notifications, notification settings, devices, sessions and login methods; the partner is left without a pair. With history it is anonymized: the name becomes "Törölt játékos", the email, phone, sex and profile picture are removed (the address is replaced by an undeliverable placeholder), as are the sessions, login methods, devices, notifications, pair links, partner search and Szervező rights; its active pair is dissolved (unplayed entries withdrawn, D19). Groups, matches and results don't change, and the account is marked `deleted_at` and left out of every list and count. Either way, the account's feedback (section 19) and usage events (section 20) are deleted. Every stored email to the address is deleted, and pending login codes and invites for it go. (The interest list and its partner notices are gone, section 17, D132: there is no signup left to delete or rewrite.) Stored mail to other people that names them goes too: unsent mail to anyone that has their address as a whole address is deleted. So is unsent mail that names other people (pair introduction, entry decision, group and match mail, a result to confirm, "holnap meccs", the automatic reminders) and has their name as whole words after the greeting, so a namesake keeps their own mail. An anonymized account gets no automatic reminder. The anonymized placeholder address never gets a login code and never logs in. The address's unsubscribe, bounce and complaint record goes too (#193), so a new registration with the address starts clean. Logging in with the address afterwards starts a new registration. Refused while a pair of theirs plays in a published group whose phase hasn't ended ("Előbb a párt vissza kell léptetni"), for the Admin's own account and for another Admin's. Szervezők don't have it. Logged as `account.delete` or `account.anonymize` with who and when, never the person's name or email. No email goes out.
  - A player deletes their own account on Profil with "Fiók törlése" at the bottom of the page (#38; decision log, 2026-11-05, D111): the same deletion as the Admin's, with the same choice between removing and anonymizing and the same refusals, worded to the player. The confirmation (an alert dialog) says plainly what happens: without history everything goes; with history the name becomes "Törölt játékos", the email, phone and logins go, and results and tables stay; a Szervező is told their rights go too; every device is logged out and the account can't be logged into again (a later login with the address starts a new registration). While a pair of theirs plays in a published group whose phase hasn't ended, the dialog says why it can't be done now ("Előbb a szervezőnek vissza kell léptetnie a párodat.") and has no delete button. The Admin can't delete their own account this way ("Adminként a saját fiókodat nem törölheted."). After the deletion the person lands logged out on the home page with "Töröltük a fiókodat, és minden eszközön kiléptettünk." Logged as `account.delete` or `account.anonymize` with the account itself as the actor (after a removal the entry has no actor, and Napló shows "Törölt játékos"). No email goes out.

## 4. Domain model (simplified)
- **City (tenant):** slug and name, managed by the platform admin at `/admin/cities` (2026-10-05). Signups, clubs, leagues and organizers belong to a city. A city marked "listed" is shown on the landing page and the public league page when it has a league; only cities with an organizer should be listed (in the MVP, Győr alone). Adding a city no longer files the earlier "Más város" signups under it, and a city's signups don't keep it from being deleted (D129, #333). User-facing text never hard-codes a city. On the landing page a city with a league shows the league's state (registration, running round, next season, or a first season still waiting for its minimum entries) and the point where a new pair can join, and leads to registration (2026-10-28, #83). The league cities' state and register button sit in the hero, above the fold. The landing page has no interest form and counts no interest signups (decision log, 2026-11-06, D123, D130, #332): a listed city without a league is not shown, and one line at the bottom ("Nincs még liga a városodban? Ha játszanál vagy szerveznél, írj nekünk: …") gives the contact address. A post of the old form is refused with a Hungarian message and stores nothing; old links (`?ref=`, `#mas-varos`) still open the page.
- **League:** city, name, organizer(s), configuration (see section 12).
- **Season:** a run of the league (e.g. "2026/27 tél", free text set by the organizer), made up of **4 phases** (12 weeks). It has a registration window and a calendar of phases, with optional breaks between phases (e.g. 21 Dec – 3 Jan); no phase runs during a break. Seasons follow each other with a 1–2 week gap, set by the organizer. The league is continuous: a season is a checkpoint (an end date, a recap, a re-entry point, division winners). Season end and switch: section 10.
  - **A league's first season may start at the minimum entries** (decision log, 2026-11-06, D124, D131, #335). On Szezonok, while the league has no season, the organizer picks "Első szezon: akkor indul, ha összegyűlik a minimum nevezés" and gives only the name and the breaks: entries open at once, with no registration deadline and no start date; while it waits it has no phases and no groups can be drafted, and no other season can be created. The league's minimum (section 12, default 12) is on the league's page, editable by the Admin and the league's Szervező while that season waits (audited `league.min_entries`); it is shown only while that season waits or before the league has any season. The write that brings the entries to the minimum (a player's "Nevezünk", the organizer's "Benevezés", or a lower minimum) fixes the start in the same D1 batch: round 1 starts on the first Monday at least 7 days after that moment (Budapest day), moved past a break by the calendar's rule (`firstSeasonStart` in the rules engine); the phases are written, and the registration window in the old sense ends the night before round 1 at 23:59. The count is checked inside the batch, so two entries at once fix it once. The date never moves again, even if entries drop below the minimum, and editing the season on Szezonok can't move round 1; entries stay open as in any season (section 4, Season entry). Every entered player (deleted accounts left out) gets one email ("Indul a szezon: nov. 23., hétfő", kind `season_start`) and one in-app and push notice with the start day and that the groups come out by that morning; the pair whose entry fixed the start gets these instead of its own entry notice. Later pairs see the date in the app. While it waits, a pair's entry notice says the start comes later. The landing card and the public league page show "9 pár nevezett, még 3 kell, utána indul."; once fixed, the landing card shows "Indul: nov. 23., hétfő" and no registration deadline. No stuck-person reminder goes out for a waiting season. Later seasons keep their own calendar (section 10).
- **Season entry:** a pair enters a season ("Nevezünk") and the entry counts at once, with no organizer approval (decision log, 2026-11-02, D62, #153). Pairs entered after the first phase has started (or once its groups are published) join at the start of the next phase, placed by the late-joiner rules (section 10). A pair whose entry has no phase left moves into the next season automatically, without re-entering. Who can enter, when, and what dissolving a pair does to its entries: decision log, 2026-10-24 (#23). On Párok the organizer (the league's Szervező or the Admin) can also enter an active pair ("Benevezés", same checks and joining phase as "Nevezünk") or take an entered pair out of the season ("Kivétel a szezonból": the entry is withdrawn and the pair left out of every later group draft, the pair stays), and enter it again later. Taking out is refused from the moment the pair's group is published (also in the days before the round starts) until the phase's deadline; in that time the pair is removed by the mid-phase withdrawal ("Visszaléptetés a csoportból", D87, #34) instead. Both players get an in-app (and push) notice, no email; both actions are audited (decision log, 2026-11-02, D63, #154). Párok also lists every registered player without an active pair in the league (name, level, whether "Párt keresek" is on, "Nincs párja"), can be searched by name (case and accents ignored) and filtered by entry ("Nevezett", "Nem nevezett", "Elutasítva", "Visszalépett", "Nincs párja", "Felbontott párok", and the shortcut "Nincs a ligában": players without a pair and pairs without an entry that counts), each option with its count; both live in the address. With a search or filter on, the Admin's "Levél nekik" opens the broadcast page with the players of the rows shown, each address once (decision log, 2026-11-02, D64, #148).
- **Venue:** the city's padel clubs, an internal list the organizer maintains, used when recording where a match was booked (and for per-club statistics). Never listed publicly; the same list feeds the poster QR codes and the optional "where do you play most" signup field (decision log, 2026-10-04).
- **Phase:** 3 weeks, back to back: the next phase starts on the Monday after the deadline, and new groups are emailed that Monday morning. Group composition is fixed within a phase. (A catch-up week exists as a league setting, off by default; revisit if the first week of a phase turns out to be lost.)
  - **The organizer can extend the running round** (decision log, 2026-11-11, D151, #356; this is the make-up week of #351). On Szezonok, the league's Szervező or the Admin picks a new deadline for the published round whose deadline hasn't passed: a Sunday 23:59 Budapest, 1–4 weeks after the current one; it can be extended again while it runs, never shortened. The confirmation shows the old and the new deadline and how many weeks later each later round starts. One D1 batch moves the round's deadline (`season_phases`) and every match's of the round (`matches.deadline`; nothing else about the matches changes), moves every later round by the same number of weeks (a round that would then touch a break starts on the first Monday after it, as the calendar does, and never before the round ahead of it ends: `extendRound` in the rules engine), forgets the "3 days before the deadline" reminder already sent for the round's unplayed matches (so it comes again), and writes the audit entry `phase.extend` with the old and new day. The deadline resolution, the round switch, the reminders and every page read these stored dates, so they follow. Every player of the round (withdrawn pairs and deleted accounts left out) gets an in-app and push notice ("Meghosszabbítottuk az 1. kört: új határidő …") and one email (section 13).
- **Group:** 4 pairs, ordered by level (1. csoport = strongest). When the number of pairs isn't divisible by 4, some groups have 3 pairs (9 = 3+3+3, 10 = 4+3+3, 11 = 4+4+3); no waiting list. 3-pair groups go at the bottom unless the organizer places them otherwise.
- **Division** (public: the bands are on the league page, section 14, and the rules explain them in `szabalyzat.md` section 1, #149): a virtual third of the ladder, named after the MAPASZ (Hungarian Padel Federation) tournament levels: **Bronz** (top), **Réz**, **Rookie** (bottom), shown as "Bronz divízió" etc. The names only borrow the federation's vocabulary: the league is not a MAPASZ tournament and gives no ranking points (decision log, 2026-10-23). Still one ladder: divisions don't change fixtures, promotion or relegation, only labels and season-end prizes (decision log, 2026-10-08).
  - Sizes from the number of groups `n` in the phase: each division gets `floor(n / 3)` groups; a remainder of 1 goes to Réz, a remainder of 2 to Réz and Rookie (15 = 5+5+5, 16 = 5+6+5, 17 = 5+6+6). With fewer than 3 groups, top-down: 1 group = Bronz; 2 groups = Bronz, Réz.
  - Recalculated every phase, so the borders move as groups are added or removed. A pair's division is the division of its current group.
  - The signup level "versenyző" has no division of its own.
- **Pair:** 2 players. A pair is a separate entity; a player can be in at most one active pair per league. Pairs are fixed within a phase: forming a new pair or changing partner is only possible between phases. No pair forms from the interest list any more (D129, #333; until then a pair named on a signup formed by itself once both registered). A player invites a partner with their **pair link** (decision log, 2026-10-31): every player without an active pair in a league has one personal link for it (`/pair/join/<token>`), shown on Kezdőlap and in Profil's Párod section ("Hívd meg a párodat", with "Link másolása" and the phone's share sheet), which they send themselves; no email. Whoever opens it sees the owner's name and the league (never an email or phone), registers or logs in (through the first-login page) and comes back to it; "Elfogadom" makes the pair (source "linkkel"), audited, while neither of the two is in an active pair of the league (checked again inside the insert). Refused: the owner's own link, a player already in a pair there, an owner who has a pair by now. The link stays the same, and works again once the owner has no pair. Every other change is an organizer task (decision log, 2026-10-25, #97): on the league's "Párok" page the Szervező (or the Admin) creates a pair from two registered players without a pair in the league, dissolves a pair ("Felbontás"), or changes one player's partner ("Partner cseréje": dissolve and new pair in one step). Dissolving or changing is refused while the pair is in a published group whose phase hasn't reached its deadline. Dissolving withdraws the entries the pair hasn't played in yet. Every change is audited. A new pair (create, or the new pair of a partner change) tells both its players in the app and by email: the partner's name, email address and phone number, the league and a link to Profil's Párod section (decision log, 2026-11-04, D102, #260; contact added by D121). Párod (Profil) shows every player their active partner's name, email address and phone number (tap to write or call), whatever made the pair (decision log, 2026-11-06, D121): partners have to reach each other, also two strangers the organizer paired. A dissolve sends nothing, and neither does a partner change to the player who leaves; the organizer tells them. Players have no dissolve or partner change of their own.
- **A league's players** (decision log, 2026-11-07, D128, #331; replaces D93): an account belongs to the leagues it joined and to every league it is in a pair of (an active or a dissolved one; an entry always belongs to such a pair), and to no other; the interest list decides nothing. It joins a league by registering or logging in through that league's register button on the landing page (`/register?league=<slug>`, the league kept in `returnTo` through the code step, Google and the first-login page; the login itself joins, before the first-login page is done; a visitor logged in already gets the card below offering that league, "Csatlakozol a ligához?", and nothing is joined until they press "Csatlakozom"), by accepting a pair link of the league, by choosing it on Kezdőlap, or by being put in a pair of it (Párok, Játékosok, the Admin's player profile; each refuses a player who isn't the league's). A registered player in no league sees one card at the top of Kezdőlap, "Melyik ligában játszol?", with one row per league of a listed city that has a season open for entries or running (city and league name, "Csatlakozom"); with one such league, that one row. Joining is audited, the account as the actor; "Csatlakozom" accepts any listed league. Kezdőlap and Párod show only the player's own leagues (seasons, pair link, "Párt keresek"); Párod without a league points to Kezdőlap. Játékosok (its rows, counts, filters and "Levél nekik"), the player choice of "Pár létrehozása", "Partner cseréje" and the profile's "Párba teszem", the substitute pick and the stuck reminders (section 13) use the rule; every list needs the first-login page done. The Admin's and the Szervezők' own accounts follow the same rule. At the release, every registered account that was a Győr player before (an account with signups: the leagues of its signups' cities, withdrawn signups too; without a signup: the leagues of Győr only), every player of a pair, and everyone with "Párt keresek" on in a league (Párkeresők, now Játékosok, listed every seeker) stays a member. Leaving a league is not possible yet.
- **Withdrawal:** if a pair drops out mid-phase (injury, moving away), the organizer withdraws it: all its matches in that phase, played or not, are removed from the group's standings, and the group continues without it. No replacement mid-phase. On Párok it is "Kivétel a szezonból" on a pair whose group is published and whose round has not ended (a running round, also before its first day), with a required reason (at most 300 characters) that only the organizer and the pair itself see (decision log, 2026-11-03, D87, #34). The pair also leaves the season, as after a take-out: it is in no later phase, and if the organizer enters it again it joins at the next phase. Its remaining matches are off: no scheduling or result step, no reminder, no auto-accept, nothing to decide; its matches show as not counting to the organizer, the group's table on Kezdőlap (the same as the public one) and on the public league page leaves it out and names it below ("Visszalépett: …"). Its players are free again (the pair can be dissolved or a partner changed, an account deleted): a pair formed from them now plays from the next phase, so no pair changes inside a running phase. The pair's players and the group's other pairs are told in the app and by push only, never by email; the other pairs get no names and no reason.
- **Substitute:** *hidden in the first season (decision log, 2026-11-08, D145): no request on the match page, no "Helyettesek" in the sidebar, no notification switch; the request and withdraw steps refuse. The code and the rules below stay for a later season.* If a player can't play (injury, illness, absence), the pair may field a substitute for one or more matches in the current phase, with the organizer's approval before the match. Rules:
  - The pair requests it, naming the substitute; the organizer sees the substitute's and the absent player's Elo side by side and approves or rejects. The opponents are notified and see the substitute on the match page.
  - The substitute needs an account (for Elo) and must not be in another pair of the same group.
  - The substitute's level should be similar to or lower than the absent player's; the organizer judges.
  - The result counts for the pair as normal (standings, set and game difference).
  - Without an approved substitute, the unplayed-match rules apply (section 8).
  - **How** (decision log, 2026-11-05, D109, #37): either player of the pair asks on the match page ("Helyettes"), naming the absent player of their pair and the substitute, before the match's agreed start (before the deadline while no time is agreed) and while it has no result. The substitute is picked by name from the league's registered players (D128, not deleted; no email shown), never one of the match's four players or a player of another pair of the group that is not withdrawn. One live (waiting or approved) substitute per absent player per match, so both players of a pair can be replaced; a person is named once per match. The asking player can withdraw a waiting request.
  - The league's organizer (or the Admin) approves or rejects it, with an optional reason (at most 300 characters, only the pair sees it), on "Helyettesek" (`/admin/leagues/:league/substitutes`), where the absent player's and the substitute's current Elo stand side by side. The organizer can revoke an approval until the match starts. Each decision is audited (D91). A waiting request lapses when the match starts. Nothing is approved automatically.
  - The match page shows approved substitutes to all four players; the pair's own requests and their state (with the reason) only to the pair. The substitute gets no notice and no access to the match page. Public pages, tables and the calendar file keep the pair's names unchanged.
  - Elo (section 11): the approved substitute is rated instead of the absent player; the match counts as a rated match for the substitute.
  - An approved substitute appearance is history for account deletion (section 3): such an account is anonymized, never removed, so who played stays known. Removing an account without history deletes its other requests as substitute; a waiting one disappears from the match page and from Helyettesek, and the pair can ask for someone else. An approval naming an anonymized account stays (shown as "Törölt játékos"); the organizer can revoke it before the match.
- **Match:** 2 pairs, a scheduled week, a deadline, a status, proposals, a result, and a venue (one of the city's clubs).
- **Proposal:** concrete time slots from one pair to the other.
- **Rating:** individual Elo per player.

- **"Párt keresek"** (decision log, 2026-11-02, D59): while a registered player has no pair in one of their leagues (D128), Profil's Párod section and Kezdőlap show a switch to ask the organizer for a partner. It is off until the player switches it on (D129, #333; the default that turned it on for a waiting solo signup went, and the release stored that default as each such player's own setting, so nobody's switch changed). Játékosok's "Párt keres" filter lists the league's registered players with it on and no pair (Párkeresők until #301, D155; its address redirects there). The organizer ticks two players without a pair and presses "Párba teszem": they become an active pair at once, with an in-app message and an email to both (D102, #260; was "no email"). A pair of two players who both have it on is stored with source "search" (D113; Párok's "Forrás": "párkeresésből"), any other "manual"; the same holds for "Pár létrehozása" on Párok and "Párba teszem" on the profile. There is no "Még nem regisztráltak" part and no pairing of solo signups any more (D129). The Admin can switch it on or off for a player on the player's profile (audited with the Admin as the actor). The "Összehozott párok" list went with Párkeresők (#301): such pairs are on Párok.
## 5. Fixtures
- Groups of 4 give 3 rounds per phase, 2 matches per round, and each pair plays once a week.
- Round order is generated automatically: week 1 is 1–4 and 2–3, week 2 is 1–3 and 2–4, week 3 is 1–2 and 3–4, based on seeding.
- Groups of 3 use the same order with seed 4 as a bye: week 1 is 2–3 (1 rests), week 2 is 1–3 (2 rests), week 3 is 1–2 (3 rests). Each pair plays 2 matches.
- Each match has a **scheduled week** (its round) and a **deadline: the end of the phase** (Sunday 23:59 of the last week). Playing in the scheduled week is strongly recommended; the UI and emails nudge towards it, but a match can be played any time until the deadline.
- There is no "home" pair: either pair may make the first proposal (section 6), so scheduling starts as soon as either side is ready.

## 6. Scheduling (core feature)
1. Either pair can make a proposal: **availability ranges ("szombat 14–18") on at least 2 different Budapest days** (D144). A match is 60 minutes and courts are booked by the hour: every range starts and ends on a whole hour and fits at least one match; starts are 07:00–22:00, so a range lies within 07:00–23:00 of one day; every range starts after now (no lead time) and its last start (end minus 60 minutes) is before the deadline. A single exact time is a range one match long. Same-day ranges that overlap or touch are joined. Optionally with a venue.
2. The other pair can:
   - **accept** one whole-hour start inside one of the ranges (from the range's start to its end minus 60 minutes, still in the future), which schedules the match, or
   - **counter-propose** with a new proposal under the same rules.
   Simply rejecting is not possible.
3. **Agreed time (decision log, 2026-10-05; one step since D148):** SMS and email are where players talk; the app keeps the evidence. The match page and Kezdőlap's next match offer "Írj mindenkinek" (D147): one SMS or email to the partner and both opponents, opened in the player's own app (the app sends nothing), its text asking to record the time here too. Either pair records the agreed slot directly, without a proposal: any future whole hour (D144) before the deadline, no lead time, no time window, optionally with a venue. It becomes **fixed by itself** at the earlier of 24 hours after it was recorded and its start, unless the other pair declines it ("Nem így beszéltük") before that, which reopens the process; the other pair can also confirm it earlier. Once fixed it is scheduled as if a slot had been accepted, and a decline changes nothing (`packages/rules` `schedulingState`, `agreedFixAt`). The cron writes the fix into the log as the system's line ("Magától fix lett…") and mirrors the match, like the result auto-accept (section 7); a player's step on the match before the cron got there writes the line first.
3a. **The tap counts (D148):** tapping "SMS mindenkinek" or "E-mail mindenkinek" sends a background POST and logs a `contacted` step (channel `sms`/`email`) for the player's pair: only one of the match's four players, only while the match can be scheduled (before the deadline, no result, not voided), at most one per pair per Budapest day; nobody is notified. The log line says only what we know: "SMS-t indított a többieknek" / "E-mailt indított a többieknek". "Számok másolása" doesn't count.
4. Once a slot is accepted, whoever books the court records which club and court they booked (manual confirmation; no SportMate integration). Either pair can record or change it, one of the city's active clubs plus an optional court; it notifies nobody (decision log, 2026-11-02, D74).
4a. **Calendar (D106, #192):** once a time is accepted or confirmed, and until the match has a result or ruling, the match page offers "Google Naptár" (a new-event link) and an .ics download: 60 minutes, the four players' names, the club and court if recorded, the match page link, never an email address or phone number. The .ics is for the match's players and the league's organizers only, has a stable UID per match and a SEQUENCE that grows with every change of time, club or court and with a cancellation, so a new download replaces the old entry; a cancelled time is STATUS:CANCELLED. No email carries it for now (the digest and "holnap meccs" emails stay off, D83, D106).
5. Every action is timestamped and logged. This is the evidence for walkovers. The log is shown on the match page to both pairs; how the state follows from it, the slot rules' details and the page: decision log, 2026-11-02 (D66, D68).
6. Either side can cancel an accepted or confirmed slot until it starts; this reopens the proposal process. The other pair gets it as a daily-digest item (section 13).
   - **Late cancellation (under 24 hours before the slot):** not an immediate walkover. The cancelling side must make the next proposal: only it gets the Teendő, but the other pair isn't blocked from proposing (D74). If the match isn't played by the deadline, the cancelling side counts as the inactive side in section 8 (walkover for the other side), regardless of its earlier activity.

## 7. Recording results
- Format (league default, one format per league, no per-match variants; see section 12):
  - Best of 2 sets. Short sets: a set goes to 4 games (win by 2); at 4-4 a regular tie-break to 7 (win by 2).
  - Valid set scores: 4-0, 4-1, 4-2, 5-3 (4-3 plays on) and, after the tie-break, 5-4. Anything else, a third set, or a super tie-break at 2-0 is rejected with a Hungarian message (`packages/rules` `validateScore`).
  - At 1-1 in sets, a super tie-break to 10 (win by 2) replaces the third set.
  - Golden point: no advantage at deuce, one point decides; the receiving pair chooses which player receives.
  - For set and game difference the super tie-break counts as one set and as a 1-0 game (ITF convention).
- Court booking: 60 minutes is usually enough (Győr clubs only take 60-minute bookings).
- Court time runs out: the match is unfinished. The pairs note the score and finish it from there in a new slot before the deadline. Only the final result is recorded.
- One pair records the result, and the other confirms or disputes it.
- **Where it was played (D146):** while the match has no club (none came with the booking, D74, and none was recorded after it), recording the result asks for one: the club is required (one of the city's active clubs), the court optional (at most 40 characters). Both go on the `result_recorded` event, and the match keeps the club (`matches.club_id`), so the match page's "Helyszín" shows it and a correction doesn't ask again. Once a club is known the result form doesn't ask, and a club sent anyway is ignored. A city with no active club can't be asked. The organizer's rulings don't ask.
- Not confirmed within 48 hours: the result is accepted automatically.
- A disputed result goes to the organizer, who decides.
- The page, corrections, the dispute's own version and the auto-accept run: decision log, 2026-11-02 (D77). The organizer's "Viták" page, decisions and overrides with a required reason: D78.

## 8. Unplayed matches (resolved automatically at the deadline)
The deadline is the end of the phase (section 5), so a match missed in its scheduled week can still be played later in the phase.
"Valid activity" means a valid proposal or counter-proposal, recording an agreed time, confirming one, or tapping "SMS mindenkinek" / "E-mail mindenkinek" on the match (D148, section 6), before the deadline. An agreed time fixed by itself is an agreed slot: both sides count as active. A match that reached an agreed slot before the deadline (scheduled at the deadline, also a scheduled slot that passed without a result) counts both sides as active, and the other side of a late cancellation counts as active; a late canceller counts as inactive whatever it did before (decision log, 2026-11-05, D103).

The system resolves a match with no result at 06:00 Budapest the morning after the deadline (Monday for the Sunday 23:59 deadline): a result recorded before then goes through confirmation, auto-accept or the organizer as usual. The outcome is stored like an organizer's ruling (marked as the deadline's) and both pairs are told in the app and by push; a withdrawn pair's matches are not resolved (D103).

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| One side active, the other did nothing (no response or no proposal) | Walkover win for the active side: 4-0 4-0 |
| Both active, no agreement | Unplayed: 0 points for both sides, no Elo change, not counted in set and game difference. The organizer may award a walkover if the log shows one side clearly blocked the match |
| Neither side active | Both sides get a loss, recorded as 0-4 0-4 for each (counts in set and game difference, 0 points) |
| Late cancellation, then not played by the deadline | The cancelling side counts as inactive: walkover for the other side (section 6) |
| Accepted slot, one side didn't show up | The side that showed up reports it, the organizer confirms, walkover (see below) |

The organizer can always override the outcome manually.

### No-show reports (decision log, 2026-11-05, D108, #33)
- **Report:** a player of either pair, on the match page ("Nem jöttek el"), once the agreed slot has started and until the deadline resolution's time (06:00 Budapest the morning after the deadline); only on a match that is scheduled, has no result recorded and counts (no withdrawn pair). Before the slot's start it is refused. One waiting report per match; the player who made it can withdraw it until the organizer decides, and then a new one can be made.
- **Decision:** the league's organizer (or an Admin) confirms or rejects it on the match's decision page (listed on "Viták"), with an optional reason both pairs see. Confirming stores a 4-0 4-0 walkover for the reporting pair as the organizer's ruling (the match final, so the tables count it) and gives a strike to both players of the other pair (source "no-show"; never a substitute). Rejecting changes nothing on the match. A match already final can't be confirmed (the organizer overrides it instead). Both are in the audit log (D91).
- **The deadline resolution** leaves a match with a waiting report alone; after a rejection the next run resolves it as usual.

### Strikes ("fekete pont", decision log, 2026-10-05)
- **A strike goes to both players of a pair** when:
  - it didn't show up at an accepted slot (organizer-confirmed no-show), or
  - it is the inactive side at the deadline: one side active and this one not, neither side active (both pairs get one), or the late-cancelling side counted as inactive (section 6).
- **No strike** when both sides were active but didn't agree, for a cancellation more than 24 hours before the slot, or for matches removed by a withdrawal.
- A substitute (section 4) never gets a strike; the pair's two players do.
- Strikes belong to the player and follow them across partner changes. They reset at the start of each season.
- **2nd strike:** a warning email to the player, unless the pair already leaves (the partner has 3); then the leaving notice instead (D103a).
- **3rd strike:** the player's pair leaves the league at the end of the current phase, not immediately, so that phase's results (including the walkovers its opponents won) stand. At the round switch, apply promotion and relegation first, then remove the pair and re-cut the groups top-down so pairs below move up into the hole (as in the season carry-over, section 10). The player's partner may form a new pair (placed as a re-formed pair). The pair may enter the next season like anyone else: no flag and no organizer approval (strikes reset at the season start; decision log, 2026-11-03, D82).
- The organizer can cancel a strike with a reason (illness, a club cancelling the court); the cancellation is logged and the count is recomputed. An override of a resolved match leaves its strikes; the organizer cancels them separately.
- Where they show: the player's own count on their season card on Kezdőlap; the counts and the season's strikes on the organizer's Párok page, with the cancel. Never on a public page (decision log, 2026-11-05, D103a).

## 9. Standings and tiebreakers
Points: win 3, loss in a played match 1, walkover loss (or a loss because neither side was active) 0.

Order within a group:
1. Points.
2. Head-to-head result (only when 2 pairs are tied).
3. Set difference.
4. Game difference.
5. Elo (average of the pair).

Set and game difference are counted over all of the pair's matches in the group in that phase, including walkovers.

When 3 or more pairs are tied, apply steps 3–5 in order. As soon as a step separates one or more pairs and only 2 are still tied with each other, go back to step 2 (head-to-head) for those 2.
Example: A beat B, B beat C, C beat A, so all have 2 wins. A has the best set difference and is 1st. B and C are still tied, and B beat C, so B is 2nd.

Pairs still equal after every step (head-to-head undecided or unplayed, same set and game difference, same Elo) keep their seed order in the group (strongest first), so the order is always strict (decision log, 2026-11-02, D61).
A withdrawn pair (section 4) is left out of the table with all of its matches; unplayed matches (section 8) give no points and count in neither difference.


## 10. Promotion and relegation
- At the end of a phase, 1st place moves up a group and last place (4th, or 3rd in a group of 3) moves down. The top group has no promotion and the bottom group has no relegation.
- **Fast calibration after the first phase of a league's first season** (decision log, 2026-10-08): seeding then rests only on self-assessed levels, so movement is faster once. At each border between two adjacent groups, **2 pairs swap** (the upper group's last two go down, the lower group's top two go up) when both groups have 4 pairs; 1 pair swaps when either group has 3. A group of 4 between two groups of 4 therefore sends its 1st and 2nd up and its 3rd and 4th down. Every later switch is 1 up / 1 down. League setting: the number of fast switches (default 1, the first switch only).
- **New pairs after the first phase** (late entries, pairs re-formed by a partner change, and returning players) start at the bottom of the division their rating points to, so they neither start at the very bottom nor push earlier pairs down for free (decision log, 2026-11-05, D107f, #12; replaces the rule of 2026-10-04):
  1. **Placement rating:** the pair's average Elo: each player's current Elo, or the level's starting Elo for a player without a rated match yet (a match as a substitute counts as rated). No discount.
  2. **Sizes first:** the next phase's group sizes are planned for the final pair count (continuing and new pairs together), by section 4: groups of 4, with 3s only at the bottom.
  3. **Entry group:** the target group is the highest group, other than the top group, whose average pair Elo is at most the pair's rating; the bottom group if there is none; with a single group, that group. The pair enters the **bottom group of the target group's division** (section 4, with the planned group count).
  4. **Free spot first:** the nearest real free spot at or below the entry group (a group with pairs and fewer than its planned size: a 3-pair group or a spot left by a leaver) takes the pair. Otherwise it goes into the entry group, and while a group is over its planned size its lowest-Elo pair moves down one group, until a group has room. A spot left above (a group short of its planned size) is filled with the best continuing pair of the group below, so the 3s stay at the bottom. Only when the continuing pairs can't fill the groups above does the best new pair move up into the hole, even across a division border, so the planned sizes hold (Peter, 2026-11-05); like a push-down, that is left to the organizer's review.
  5. Several new pairs are placed one by one, highest rating first, after promotion and relegation of the continuing pairs and after the leavers are removed.
  6. The result is part of the round switch draft; the organizer can move pairs before publishing. A pair pushed down across a division border by step 4 is left to this review (no extra rule).
- First phase of a season: the system sorts the entered pairs by average Elo (no discount, no cap) into groups of 4 (and 3, section 4); the organizer can move pairs between groups, then publishes. Publishing creates the phase's matches, emails every player and locks the groups (decision log, 2026-10-24, #24).
- **Round switch:** after the deadline (Sunday 23:59) the system resolves unplayed matches (section 8, from Monday 06:00) and drafts the next phase's groups (promotion, relegation, newly entered pairs). The organizer reviews it, settles open disputes and publishes on Monday morning; publishing sends the new-phase emails (decision log, 2026-11-05, D107, #12, #35):
  - Promotion and relegation run on the final tables, so a pair withdrawn mid-phase is out of them and the table's last goes down; at a fast switch a group counts by its table. A result recorded but not yet confirmed counts as recorded; a disputed one doesn't count.
  - The draft is made once, as soon as no counting match of the ending phase waits for the resolution. It is never overwritten by the system. On Csoportok the organizer sees each pair's last group and place (or "Új pár"), the pairs that leave and why, and the disputed results. They can move pairs, rebuild the draft from the current results ("Tervezet újraszámolása", which drops the moves), and publish once no disputed result and no waiting no-show report (section 8) is left. A draft whose results or pairs have changed since it was made (who moves up or down, who leaves, who is new) must be rebuilt before it can be published.
  - The last phase of a season has no next-phase draft (season switch below).
- **Season end and switch** (decision log, 2026-10-05):
  1. **Final standings:** each pair's group and place in the last phase.
  2. **Winners** (decision log, 2026-10-08), from the last phase's standings, before the last promotion and relegation:
     - **Divízió győztes:** 1st place of each division's top group (with 15 groups: groups 1, 6 and 11). Only the division winners get a prize, one per pair, one prize per division present: with 1 group only Bronz has a winner (1 prize), with 2 groups Bronz and Réz (2 prizes), with 3 or more groups all three (3 prizes); the other group winners get none (decision log, 2026-11-03, D81).
     - **Eligibility:** both players of the pair played in at least 2 phases of the season as a pair member (substitute appearances don't count; the 2 phases may be with different partners). If an ineligible pair wins a division's top group, the result and promotion stand, and the prize goes to that group's best-placed eligible pair (none if there is none). The group card shows the ineligible pair as "Ebben a szezonban még nem díjjogosultak". This keeps a strong pair that joins in the last phase with an understated level from taking a prize, without anyone judging the players.
     - The prizes and their sponsors are set by the organizer per season and division, never named in the rules (free text, e.g. "Felajánlja: Padel Club Győr"); the app shows them, nothing is paid or handed out in the app. Prizes are handed over at a short meeting at the sponsor club after the season (group photo, no matches), arranged by the organizer with the club.
  3. **Re-entry:** during the next season's registration window, continuing pairs click "Nevezünk újra"; the organizer can also re-enter a pair by hand. Pairs that don't re-enter leave the league. A pair entered for a season it never played in (no phase left when it entered) moves into the next season automatically.
  4. **Carry-over draft:** apply the last phase's promotion and relegation as at a round switch. List the resulting groups top-down, each group's pairs ordered by last-phase group, then place. Remove pairs that didn't re-enter and pairs whose players changed. Re-cut the remaining order top-down into groups of the section 4 sizes, so pairs below move up into the holes. Then place new pairs and re-formed pairs (partner change) by the late-joiner rules above.
  5. The organizer adjusts the draft and publishes it on the Monday the new season starts; publishing sends the new-phase emails.
  6. **Elo carries over** unchanged; no reset or decay.
  7. **Season archive and recap:** see sections 13 and 14.
- Pairs removed by strikes (section 8) leave at the round switch, after promotion and relegation; pairs below move up into the hole.
- Pairs leaving mid-season and empty spots: the organizer fills them from the waiting list; the rules engine suggests a placement.
- Pair count not divisible by 4: groups of 3 fill the gap (section 4).

## 11. Rating (Elo)
- Individual Elo, starting value based on self-assessed level (kezdő 1000, középhaladó 1200, haladó 1400, versenyző 1600; the same levels as the signup form).
- Pair rating = average of the two players.
- After a match, both players receive the same delta. K = 40 for a player's first 10 rated matches, 20 after (decision log, 2026-10-05). A side's K is the average of its two players' K (40, 30 or 20), so a match is zero-sum only when both sides' K are equal (D104).
- Rated matches: played matches that are final (a confirmed result, the 48-hour auto-accept, an organizer's ruling with a played score). Walkovers, both-inactive and unplayed matches don't change Elo.
- Ratings are a replay of every rated match from the starting values, in the order the matches first became final. An organizer's later ruling replays its match in place, so the ratings end up as if the new outcome had been there from the start (D104).
- A pair withdrawn mid-phase keeps the changes of its final matches; its matches not yet final are never rated. No replay on withdrawal (D104).
- Stored unrounded; the profile shows the rounded number, organizer tables one decimal.
- Substitutes: Elo is computed from the players who actually played. The substitute's rating changes, the absent player's doesn't.

## 13. Notifications (in Hungarian; decision log, 2026-10-05)
Channels: the in-app list, email and web push (PWA). Every user chooses on the profile (Profil → Értesítések) whether notifications also come by email, by push, or both; both switches are on by default (decision log, 2026-10-28, #115, D50). No player gets more than one non-urgent email a day.

Emails are sent from `hello@padelparbaj.hu` through Google Workspace's SMTP relay; what the relay refuses or can't take goes through Resend. Each email is marked sent or failed on its own. The league's own limit is 5,000 emails a day with the relay (100 without it, on Resend alone), sent in this order: login codes, personal emails, bulk mail (decision log, 2026-10-28, #111; 5,000 since 2026-10-29, #127, D51). While the relay is not set up, "Kimenő levelek" says so in production.

Every email goes out by itself as soon as it is made, within the daily limit; what doesn't fit waits for the next day's limit, in the send order above. Nothing waits for an Admin. Bulk mail (registration invites, broadcasts) is only queued by the Admin's click and sent right after the page answers, in the background; personal emails (login codes, signup confirmations, entry decisions, group emails, pair intros) go out inside their request (decision log, 2026-11-02, #140, D72). "Kimenő levelek" (`/admin/outbox`, Admin only) is the log: per kind, how many emails wait, went out and failed, with the newest failure's reason, never an address (decision log, 2026-10-28, #112; replaces the held outbox of #88).

**A person's email log** (decision log, 2026-11-11, D157, #242): a section, "Levelek", of the Admin's player profile (section 2, D156; Admin only, like the profile). It lists every email stored for the person's address, newest first (the newest 100, with the total): when it was made and when it went out (Budapest time), the kind with the names of "Kimenő levelek", the subject, how it went out ("Google-relé" or "Resend"), and its state: "Sorban vár", "Küldés alatt", "Nem ment ki" (with the failure's reason), "Elküldve", "Kézbesítve", "Visszapattant" (for good), "Átmenetileg visszapattant", "Spamnek jelölte". Resend's webhook events (`email.delivered`, `email.bounced`, `email.complained`) mark the email they name, by the id Resend gave it (stored when sent), with the event's time: a stronger state stays when a weaker one comes later (temporary bounce < delivered < permanent bounce < complaint). The relay reports nothing back, so a relay email reads "Elküldve" with "A Google-relé nem jelzi vissza a kézbesítést.", unless a bounce for it becomes known. If the address is on the do-not-mail list, the section says why and since when. Subjects and failure reasons have every address hidden, so only the person's own address (in the profile's facts) is on the page. A deleted or anonymized account's stored mail is gone (section 3), so there is nothing to show (and no profile). The interest list is gone (D129), so the log opens from the profile only; Felhasználók's search by name or address finds the account. Flag for human review: personal data, delivery events tied to stored emails.

**Email opens** (decision log, 2026-11-12, D161, #243): every email except a login code carries a tracking image, through the relay and Resend alike (Peter, 2026-11-04). Each stored email gets its own random token when it is queued (128 random bits; never an id or an address); when it is sent, its HTML gets a 1x1 image at `/email/open/<token>`. A text-only mail client can't load it, and a test broadcast (sent only to the Admin, never stored) has none. The address answers every request, known token or not, with the same transparent 1x1 GIF that no one may cache; a known token marks its email: the first open's time (kept) and one more open. Nothing else is recorded: no IP address, device, browser or account; the request log shows the path with the token hidden. In "Levelek" a sent, tracked email reads "Megnyitva: <first open, Budapest time> · <n> megnyitás", else "Nem ismert, hogy megnyitotta"; a login code, an email that hasn't gone out and one from before the tracking show nothing. One note above the list says opens are approximate: some mail apps load images by themselves (counted as opened without reading), others block them (read but not counted). The open data lives on the stored email and goes with it (account deletion, section 3, and every other cleanup); no separate retention. Unsubscribed or blocked addresses get no bulk mail, so no tracking either. Flag for human review: email, personal data.

**Rules for all notifications:**
- Every notification has a one-tap action link (accept, confirm, record a time) and a shareable link ("küldd el a párodnak / ellenfélnek").
- The home screen shows the same open items as a "Teendők" list (decision log, 2026-11-02, D67).

A tap on "SMS mindenkinek" / "E-mail mindenkinek" tells nobody (D148).
- **The cron's run limits** (D84, #183): the cron runs every 10 minutes; one run may make 50 database queries and 50 outgoing calls (each push to a device, each email connection; Workers Free). Its steps, in order: login code cleanup (D99, #168), auto-accept, deadline resolution (D103), round switch draft (D107), match reminders and digest, stuck reminders, the one-time interest list deletion (D132, #334; nothing once it is done), pushes, emails, push stats (D100); a failed step is logged under its name and the next one runs (#185, #188). The reminders' items and their emails are stored together with the record that they were made, in one transaction; their pushes wait in a queue and go out oldest first, as many as fit a run, so a large league gets them over a few runs (200 players with 150 devices: by about 08:00). One queued item goes to at most 10 of the player's devices, the newest first (D97, #222); the player's older devices are forgotten when the cron pushes to them, and logged once per run (D116, #279). What a run leaves is logged and done by the next one; nothing goes out twice.
- **Digest and reminders** (decision log, 2026-11-02, D75): the cron makes the day's digest reminders at 07:00 Budapest (pushed from then on, D84) and right after sends the digest, once a day (a morning no run finished by noon is finished by the next run before noon, D84) (off for now, D83; `MATCH_REMINDER_EMAILS` in `match-reminders.ts`): one email per player with the digest items (`DIGEST_KINDS`) of the last day not opened in the app yet; none on a day without such items, none with the email switch off. "2 days into the week" is Wednesday, "3 days before the deadline" the Thursday before the deadline's Sunday, both Budapest calendar days; the end-of-week reminder is only for a match not scheduled yet, never for week 3. "Holnap meccs" goes out in the first run from 18:00 to 21:00 sharp the day before (in the app and by push; its email is off for now, D83). Each reminder once per match (per slot for "holnap meccs"), none for a played or resolved match.
- Every notification is also kept in the app's notification list (`/notifications`), whatever the switches: unread ones first, opening one marks it read. Szervezők and Admins can switch off their own kinds (e.g. a disputed result) on the profile page; the new-signup notice went with the interest list (D129, #333); a kind switched off is not stored or sent at all. Players have no per-kind switches, only the two channels.
- **Email switch:** off, no email goes to that account's address except login codes (signup confirmations went with the interest list, #334): broadcasts, new pairs' emails and stuck reminders are skipped too (Peter, 2026-10-29). Addresses without an account get everything as before. (Registration invites, partner notices and pair intros went with the interest list, #333.)
- **Push switch:** off, no push goes to any of the account's devices. Turning it on asks this browser for permission and subscribes this device; other devices are switched on from themselves ("Bekapcsolom ezen az eszközön"). On iPhone it needs iOS 16.4+ and the site added to the home screen; the profile explains this. A player who switches email off gets instant items by push and in the app only.
- **Feedback sends nothing** (decision log, 2026-11-04, D94): a feedback message, a prompt answer or a new bug-report issue creates no email, no push and no notification, for the sender or the Admin. The Admin finds feedback on "Visszajelzések" (section 19).

## 19. Feedback (decision log, 2026-11-04, D94, #138)
Players and visitors can tell us from the app that something is broken or could be better, and every user is asked once how it is going. Nothing here sends mail, push or a notification, to anyone (section 13).

**Form ("Visszajelzés").** Linked from the sidebar of every logged-in page and from the footer of the public pages; the page is `/feedback`, public.
- Fields: a type (`Hiba`, `Ötlet`, `Egyéb`) and a required message (1–2000 characters). No email address and no name: **we never reply by email**, so the form shows the contact address (the organizer's, as on the privacy page) for anyone who wants an answer.
- Added automatically, and shown to the sender above the button ("Ezt küldjük el veled: ..."): the page the form was opened from (path only, no query string), the time, the device class (mobile or desktop) and browser family, the app version, and the account when logged in. The full browser string and the IP address are not stored.
- After sending: a short thank-you in place of the form, with a link back to where the sender came from. Nothing else on the page changes.
- Logged-out visitors can send feedback too, behind the same bot check as the signup form (Turnstile, section 3's signup). A failed check or a rate limit gives a short Hungarian error and stores nothing.
- Rate limit (default, Peter may change it): a logged-in account 5 messages an hour and 20 a day; logged-out visitors together 30 an hour. The limit counts stored rows, so it needs no IP address.
- The form tells people not to write passwords or other people's personal data.

**Stored.** One row per message: type (`bug`, `idea`, `other`, or `rating` for a prompt answer), message, rating (1–5, `rating` only), page, device class, browser, app version, the account (empty for visitors), the time, "done" (time and who marked it), the GitHub issue number (bugs only). Feedback is kept 1 year, then a daily cron step deletes it; deleting an account deletes its feedback with it (the anonymizing delete too, since a message may name its sender).

**Admin view ("Visszajelzések", Admin only).** Newest first, filter by type and by open or done, open one to read it with its context and the sender (the account's name, never shown in a GitHub issue), mark it done or open again.

**Bug reports and GitHub.** Only `Hiba` messages open an issue, in this private repo, with the bug label. The issue has a fixed title (`Hiba a visszajelzésből #<n>`), the message text, the page, the time, the device and browser, the app version and a link to the item in the admin view. **No name, no email, no phone number and no account id.** (The message is free text, so the form's warning above matters; an issue with personal data in it is edited by the Admin by hand.) The token is a fine-grained GitHub token that can only write issues in this repo (secret `GITHUB_TOKEN`; local dev logs the issue instead of opening it). The issue is opened right after the message is stored, in the background; if GitHub can't be reached or the token is missing, the message stays stored without an issue number and a cron step opens it later (failure-isolated, section 13, within the run limits, a few issues per run). `Ötlet`, `Egyéb` and prompt answers open no issue.

**Feedback prompt.** Every logged-in user except the Admin (players and Szervezők) is asked **once, ever**.
- A **use** is a Budapest calendar day on which the account opened an app page logged in; many visits on one day count once. The account keeps its number of uses and the day of the last one.
- The prompt shows from the first use day **after the account has had 4 earlier use days**, so never on the first four, and only on Kezdőlap (never on a form, never on another page).
- A small card on Kezdőlap, not a modal: "Hogy tetszik eddig a Padelpárbaj?", five stars (1–5), an optional comment, "Küldés", a close button and "Most nem". Tapping a star opens the comment box and "Küldés"; nothing is sent before "Küldés".
- Sending, closing and "Most nem" all end it: the account's prompt state is set (time and outcome), so it never shows again on any device. A rating is stored as feedback of type `rating` and opens no issue.
- No email, no push, no notification (section 13).

**Out of scope:** screenshots or attachments, chat or replies in the app, a reminder to give feedback, per-round prompts.

## 20. Usage statistics (decision log, 2026-11-04, D95, #139)
The Admin sees how players use the app. No cookie, no local storage, no consent banner, no third-party tracker: everything is measured on our server and stays in our database. A Cloudflare Web Analytics script is not added to the logged-in pages; the public pages keep what they have (decision log, 2026-10-04, #45).

**What is recorded.** One event row per page view or action: the account, the time, the kind (`view` or the action's name), the page (the route's name, e.g. `match`, never the full path or any id), the device class (mobile or desktop), the browser family and whether the visit came from the installed app (the home-screen icon opens the app at a start address with a marker; the marker is stored on the event of that load, and a visit is "telepített app" when its first event carries it, else "böngésző". An installed app resumed from the background without reloading the start address starts a visit that counts as "böngésző": a known undercount, accepted for now). No IP address, no full browser string, no query string, no message or score text.
- Recorded: every page view in the logged-in app (document and in-app navigation) and these actions: times offered, time accepted, result recorded, result confirmed, a notification switch changed, push enabled on a device, a pair link created, a "Párt keresek" post. More actions are added by listing them in one place.
- **Not recorded: Szervezők and Admins.** An account that is the Admin or a Szervező of any league has no events written, and reports also leave out accounts that are one now (an account made Szervező later disappears from the numbers of its past too).
- Recording runs after the response is sent (the background helper, so no page waits for it) and any failure is logged and ignored: a page never breaks or slows because of it.
- Kept 180 days (about a season with its break), then a daily cron step deletes older events; deleting an account deletes its events.

**Visit.** A player's events with no pause longer than **30 minutes** between two of them (default, Peter may change it). Visit length = last event minus first event; a visit with one event has no length and is left out of the length statistics. Time on a page = the time to the next event in the same visit, capped at 5 minutes; the last page of a visit has none.

**Admin view ("Használat", Admin only).** `/admin/usage`, a date range in the page address (`?from=&to=`, default the last 14 days, at most the 180 kept days). Totals and simple bars, no chart library, no list of players, no screen with one player's history.
1. **Active players** per day, per week and per round (a phase of the current or chosen season, by its dates); a player is active with at least one visit in the period.
2. **Visits:** visits per active player, pages per visit, typical (median) visit length, typical time on the main pages.
3. **Flows with drop-off:** per step, the number of people who reached it and the share of the previous step.
   - Join: landing page views (all sources, from the landing views: views, not people) → accounts created in the range → first login finished → profile complete (phone and level) → entry submitted. Anyone can register without a signup on the interest list (decision log, 2026-10-28, #113), so the cohort is the accounts; the steps after the first count accounts, each of the same cohort. The landing views step is only a hint. Nothing is taken from the interest list (D129, #333: the two side figures from it are gone).
   - Match (counted per match, not per person): the matches that had a time offered in the range → time accepted → result recorded → result confirmed. A result accepted automatically after 48 hours counts as confirmed (section 7).
   - Steps are read from the existing data (accounts, profiles, entries, the match log), not from usage events, so they cover the time before usage recording started. They are exact for the accounts and matches that still exist: a deleted account drops out of its steps (section 3).
4. **Feature use:** how many times each recorded action was used in the range, and by how many distinct players, split by mobile and desktop.
5. **Devices:** share of mobile and desktop, of browser families, and of installed app against browser, by visit.

**Privacy (D95).** Usage per account is personal data. The legal basis is the organizer's legitimate interest in improving the service; it is first-party, collects nothing from the device and shares nothing with third parties, so no consent is asked. A player may object by emailing the contact address; the Admin then switches the measurement off for that account ("Használat mérése: ki" on "Felhasználók"), which deletes its events and records no more. The privacy page says what is measured and for how long (section 17). **Flag for human review.**

**Out of scope:** ad pixels and campaign attribution (the `?ref=` tracking stays), session recordings, heatmaps, A/B tests, email opens or clicks, a per-player history, the feedback form (section 19).
